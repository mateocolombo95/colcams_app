# Product definition

## Working concept

A technical-commercial copilot for security installers.

The installer uses the web app during a site survey with the customer. The app captures requirements, documents the site, dimensions the solution, calculates materials/labor/cost/margin, and turns that into a commercial proposal.

The core object is a **Project**, not a Quote.

A project may contain:

- Customer
- Site
- Survey
- Requirements
- Photos / plans
- Technical design
- BOM
- Suppliers
- Price snapshots
- Labor
- Costs
- One or more commercial proposals
- Revisions

## Primary user

Independent installers and small/medium security integrators.

## Initial vertical

CCTV IP.

Future verticals:

- CCTV Wi-Fi
- Access control
- Intrusion alarms
- Intercom
- Networks / Wi-Fi
- Fire alarm

## Product promise

Reduce the time between the site visit and a professional quote while reducing technical sizing and pricing mistakes.

## Guiding principles

1. Critical engineering calculations must be deterministic and auditable.
2. AI may assist with data capture, interpretation and writing, not silently decide critical sizing.
3. Costs shown to the installer are separate from commercial information shown to the customer.
4. A quote stores a snapshot of the prices used at that time.
5. The installer must always be able to manually override a recommendation.
